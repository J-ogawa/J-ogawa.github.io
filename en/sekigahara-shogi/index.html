SekigaharaShogi

A new style of shogi where you build your own army

Download on the App Store

How to Play

  • Selection: choose your pieces within a fixed cost (up to 27 pieces, up to 9 pawns, and at least one king)
  • Placement: place your pieces wherever you like. The second player can see the opponent's army before choosing a formation
  • Battle: the rules are almost the same as shogi. If your opponent has more than one king, capture them all to win

Game Modes

  • Online: play anyone in the world with Game Center and climb the leaderboard
  • Versus computer: take on opponents with all kinds of formations, and level up to unlock more pieces
  • 6×6 board: two players can play Sekigahara Shogi on one device
  • 9×9 board: use it as a regular shogi board
