DressUpTimer

A timer you can dress up any way you like

Features

  • Use a color or your own picture as the background (with zoom and position adjustment)
  • Backgrounds that change as time goes by
  • Choose the font, size, and color of the countdown (you can import font files, too)
  • Alarm sounds from your own recordings, videos, or audio files
  • Pomodoro mode (repeating work and break intervals)

FAQ

Q. Where are my timers saved?

Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes your timers and the pictures, sounds, and fonts you added.

Q. About ads

The app is free and supported by ads. Ads are never shown while a timer is running.
